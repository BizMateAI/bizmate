Common questions

How does BizMate help my business?
BizMate bundles scheduling, lead capture, local listings, and follow-up automations into a simple system that converts more customers and automates repetitive tasks.
How quickly will I see results?
Most customers see measurable improvements in 30–90 days. The faster core integrations (scheduling, listings, campaigns) are enabled, the quicker you’ll see ROI.
What integrations do you support?
Calendly, Brevo (email), Stripe, Google Business Profile, Google Sheets, Zapier and more. We add integrations regularly — request any specific connector during consultation.
How does onboarding work?
We run a short intake call, configure your workflows, test key flows and train staff. Bronze is minimal setup; Silver and Gold include more hands-on onboarding sessions.
Is my customer data secure?
Yes. We use TLS encryption in transit, secure third-party APIs for integrations, and follow reasonable access controls. Contact us for enterprise-level security requirements.
What support is included?
Email support for all plans, priority support for Silver, and dedicated success contact for Gold customers. Response SLAs are outlined in plan details.
Can I pay monthly or annually?
Both options are available. Annual plans include a discount and priority onboarding. Payments are handled securely via Stripe.
Do you offer trials or pilots?
Yes — pilots are available for qualified local businesses (typically a 30-day engagement to prove value). Ask about pilots during your demo.
How do I cancel?
Monthly subscriptions can be canceled anytime from the account dashboard or by contacting support. Enterprise contracts vary — review your agreement.
How do I get started?
Book a free 30-minute consultation at /consultation.html or use our Calendly link on the same page. We'll assess fit and propose a quick starter plan.
