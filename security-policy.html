Security Policy

Last updated: October 2025

BizMate prioritizes the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of all client and user data. This policy outlines our commitment to maintaining secure systems and handling vulnerabilities responsibly.

1. Security Practices

  • All data transmitted between users and our servers is protected by SSL/TLS encryption.
  • We use industry-standard authentication and access controls for administrative systems.
  • Backups are encrypted and stored securely.

2. Data Storage

Client data is processed through trusted third-party services such as Formspree, Google, and Calendly. These services comply with global data protection standards (GDPR, PIPEDA, CCPA).

3. Vulnerability Reporting

We welcome responsible disclosure of any security vulnerabilities. Please contact our security team at security@bizmte.com. We aim to respond within 72 hours.

4. Incident Response

In case of a security breach, affected users will be notified promptly with details of the impact, mitigation steps, and prevention measures.

5. Continuous Improvement

Security practices are reviewed quarterly to align with the latest standards and technologies.
